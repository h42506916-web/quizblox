The guide

How QuizBlox works

A short guide for teachers and students: modes, scoring and tips. No exam at the end.

Overview

Each game uses one question set. Questions come in three flavours: multiple choice (two to four answers), true/false, and type-the-answer, where capitals and small typos are forgiven. Any question can carry an emoji picture clue. You play against four simulated opponents, and the game ends when time runs out or someone reaches the goal score. Afterwards you see a podium, the final leaderboard and your own stats.

  1. Pick a set on the Play page, or build one in Question Sets.
  2. Choose a mode, time limit, goal and opponent difficulty.
  3. Answer questions with the mouse, touch, or the keys 1–4.

Game modes

Gold Quest

Answer a question correctly to open one of three chests. You choose without knowing what is inside. Wrong answers earn nothing, but your gold is safe from your own mistakes – only chests, goblins and sneaky opponents can take it. A streak of 5, 10 or 15 (and so on) earns a lucky key (you can hold two). If the chest you pick is a dud, you can spend a key to reroll it into a good one, or keep the dud. Rivals can earn and use keys too, so a long Gold Quest stays a fair fight. Open three good chests in a row and they glow on a hot streak (pure juice — the gold inside is unchanged).

Chest resultEffect
Gold+20 to +110 gold
Jackpot+160 to +260 gold (rare)
Double goldAdds your current gold again, up to +300
StealTake 25–70 gold from a bot
Lose goldLose 15–40 gold, or 25% of your gold
EmptyNothing happens. Much like your gold.

Bots can also steal from you, so a lead is never completely safe. Each set of three chests always includes at least one good pick.

Quiz Race

A straightforward points race. Every correct answer scores, with bonuses for building a streak and answering quickly. A pack track on the leaderboard shows everyone's place, how far you are from the lead, and your current streak bonus. Scores are unchanged.

Vault Heist

Every correct answer gives you a shot at the vault. A marker slides back and forth along a lock bar; press Space (or the button) to stop it. The green zone shrinks and the marker speeds up as the game goes on, but a streak makes the zone a little wider. A security line (Low, Medium or High) tracks how tight the lock has become, high security draws a laser grid, a near miss gets a close-call toast, and three perfect picks in a row earn a Master Thief banner. None of that changes the cash math.

Where you stopResult
Dead centre+90 to +140 cash, plus 25–45 swiped from the richest rival
Green zone+40 to +70 cash
Just outside+15 cash for effort
Way off (or too slow)Alarm! Lose 15–35 cash

Lucky Catch

A correct answer hooks a fish. Tap the reel button (or press Space) over and over to fill the reel meter before the fish escapes. The meter drains while you rest, and heavier fish drain it faster. Streaks make big fish more likely. A depth line tells you if the bite is near the surface or in deep water; shark-size (and bigger) bites get a “big one” banner, the pond can lunge mid-reel, and landing one with almost no time left earns a close-call toast. None of that changes the catch math.

Café Rush

A correct answer brings a customer to the counter. Their order shows as a row of emoji; tap the matching items on the tray (or press 1–4) in the same order. Orders start at two items and grow to three, then four. A wrong tap costs you about half a second of the customer's patience. If the patience bar runs out they storm off and leave a bad review. Land three perfect orders and a regular starts dropping by for “the usual” — a flavour bonus, not extra coins. A perfect-streak line under the tray keeps count.

ResultCoins

Monster Brawl

A boss with a health bar appears, and every correct answer lets you attack it. Choose a move (press 1, 2 or 3). Move cards show the damage you will deal with your current streak (and a doubled Power Up). If a jab or smash would knock the boss out, the card says FINISH IT! and a finishing-blow banner fires on the hit. The numbers themselves are unchanged. The boss's health is shared: your hits and the bots' hits all count against it. The boss's HP is the “goal” you choose, and the player who deals the most damage wins. If the clock runs out first, the boss survives and the most damage still wins. Wrong answers do no damage but never cost you any.

MoveDamage
🗡️ Quick Jab25, always
💥 Mega Smash65, but only a 60% chance to hit
⚡ Power Up15, and your next attack does double
Combo bonus+4 damage per answer in your streak (up to +24)

Boss phases. When a boss drops to 25% health (or, in a game with no HP goal, when the last quarter of the clock begins) it becomes enraged: it glows red and shakes. Every second question after that it winds up a special attack. A warning appears under its name for about two seconds, then either your answers shuffle or 3 seconds drain from the question timer (never below 1.5 seconds left). Each boss has its own special names and one-liners. Special attacks never fire in “no timers” mode, and typed or true/false questions only get the time drain.

Team brawl in class mode. Choose Monster Brawl as the class game mode and set a class boss HP (5,000, 10,000 or 20,000). Students play from the class link as usual, and every student’s damage adds up. When you paste result codes, the teacher page shows one shared boss health bar, who has dealt the most damage and a “defeated by the class” message once the total passes the boss’s HP. The boss is the same one students meet in their game and it is included on the printed results.

Rocket Race

Five rockets climb a vertical track towards space. Every correct answer fuels your rocket (+2 km) and lets you play a card (press 1, 2 or 3). The goal is your target altitude in kilometres, and the first rocket to reach it wins. Wrong answers do nothing but never cost you altitude. The bots play cards too, and they will sabotage you if you lead, so a Shield is worth having. As you climb, the track marks altitude bands — Launch pad, Upper air, Orbit and Space — and crossing a new band gets a short banner. The bands are just a progress readout; they do not change how many kilometres a card is worth.

CardEffect
🚀 Boost+10 km, plus 2 km for each answer in your streak (up to +8). If you are within 12 km behind another rocket, you are in the slipstream and Boost gets +4 km extra
🛡️ Shield+4 km, and the next sabotage aimed at you is blocked
🌀 Sabotage+2 km, and the rocket currently in the lead (other than you) loses 8 km, unless it has a shield
Fuel+2 km on every card you play

All-modes tournament

A short playlist of every mode, in this order: Gold Quest, Quiz Race, Vault Heist, Lucky Catch, Café Rush, Monster Brawl and Rocket Race. Each leg is 30 seconds with no score goal, and you keep the same four rivals throughout. After each leg you get placement points (5 for 1st, down to 1 for 5th). Those points decide the final podium, so a strong Gold Quest cannot be compared with kilograms of fish. Start from the home page, the Play page, or play.html?tourney=1. Daily, weekly, class, demo, mix, challenge and Pass & Play games stay as they are. One finished tournament counts as one game in your stats.

The time limit is the length of the whole game (30 seconds to 5 minutes). The goal ends the game early for whoever reaches it first. Each question also has its own answer timer, which you can set per question in the editor (default 20 seconds; 0 means no limit).

Game feel

On Fire kicks in when you get five answers in a row. The board glows, the streak pill burns hotter, and banners pop at 5 (On Fire), 8 (Unstoppable) and 10 (Legendary). It is pure celebration — your score does not change.

Final Lap is the last 15 seconds of a timed game (30 seconds or longer). The clock turns urgent, the music picks up, a Final Lap banner flashes once, and you get a neck-and-neck toast if you are close to the lead.

Photo finish: if the clock hits zero and you are tied for first with at least one bot (and someone has scored), the game does not end. You get one sudden-death question with no minigame. A correct answer is worth +1, which breaks the usual tie-break (tied scores otherwise put you behind the bots) and gives you first place. Miss it, or let the question timer run out, and the tie stands.

Vault Heist, Lucky Catch and Café Rush also have a little extra juice on a perfect lock, a big catch or a perfect order. Reduced-motion settings keep the banners as toasts and skip the flashes.

Opponents

Opponents are simulated players with their own accuracy and speed. Easy opponents are slower and less accurate, Hard opponents answer faster and miss less often. They don't see the questions – they are there to make the leaderboard move.

Each rival has a personality: Captain Quill is a steady veteran, Prof. Pickle is rarely wrong but slow, Zippy is fast and sloppy, Nova heats up near the end, Mochi naps until halfway, Turbo is slow and very accurate, Bubbles gets better on a streak and Sir Squiggle never stops talking. They trash-talk in a speech bubble (turn it off in Settings). Hard mode is an optional extra: question timers are 30% shorter, rivals are a little sharper and wrong answers cost 5% of your score.

Challenge links

After any game, choose Challenge a friend to get a link that carries your score, the set, the mode and the time. Whoever opens it plays the same game and finds out whether they beat you. Nothing is uploaded; it all travels inside the link.

Mastery stars

Every question set shows up to three ★ stars. On sets with levels you earn one star for each level (Easy, Medium, Hard) where you answer 75% or more correctly over at least 4 recent answers, counting games and practice. Sets without levels earn stars from overall accuracy: ★ for 6 answers at 70%, ★★ for 12 at 80%, ★★★ for 20 at 90%. Only your recent answers count (older ones fade), so stars can be lost as well as won. Hover a star row for the breakdown, or open Stats.

Daily mix

A quick one-and-a-half minute Quiz Race made of 12 questions from lots of different sets, ramping from Easy to Hard. It is the same for you all day, and finishing it earns the Mixmaster achievement. Random mix gives a different one every time.

Study cards

Want a quieter look at a set before the bots show up? Open Study cards from the Play page, a Question Sets row, or the end of a game (for the questions you missed). Flip a card to see the answer, then mark it I know this or Still learning. At the end you can practise just the shaky ones, flip the deck again, or start a real game. There is no clock and no score. Finishing a run of 8 or more cards unlocks the Card sharp achievement. Keyboard: Space flips, arrows move, 1 and 2 mark a card, Esc goes back.

Play streak

Any finished game counts for the day (your local date). A little week of pips on the home, play and stats pages shows the last seven days. Keep a run going: three days in a row unlocks On a roll. Miss a full day and the current streak resets, but your best streak stays on Stats.

Set of the week

Each week QuizBlox highlights one built-in set and pairs it with a mode that fits the theme (Science Week, Story Time, Number Crunch and so on). Play it from the home or Play page as a 3-minute event. Your result is remembered for the week, like the daily challenge.

Class mode

Go to Class mode, name your class, pick a set, mode and time, and copy the student link. Students open it, type their name and play one game. At the end they see a short result code (it starts with QB1.) that they send back to you by chat, email or a note. Paste any number of codes into the teacher page to build a class leaderboard (each student’s best game counts), with highlights for top score, sharpshooter and hot streak. Open Classroom view to project a big podium and board (you can paste more codes there), Reuse class to start a fresh challenge with the same settings, download a CSV for a spreadsheet or print it. Everything stays in the browsers involved: there is no server, so it works offline too. Codes are checked for typos and cut-off text but are not tamper-proof, so use them for fun and review rather than marking.

Tips for teachers

  • Keep sets to 10–30 questions so students see variety in a 3-minute game.
  • Use per-question timers for quick recall, and 0 (no limit) for problems that need working out.
  • Duplicate a built-in sample set to use it as a template, or paste a worksheet, CSV or GIFT quiz into Bulk add (you will see a preview before the questions are added).
  • Flip through a set with Study cards on the board before a game, and practise just the shaky ones.
  • Project the game on a screen and let students call out answers, or run it on shared devices in small groups.
  • Print a worksheet, an exit ticket, or Version A and B sheets from Question Sets, and preview them on screen first. Optional lines for class, teacher and period, plus difficulty marks, are there if you want them.
  • After you paste results, open Classroom view to put a big leaderboard on the projector, and use Reuse class when the next period needs the same set and rules with a clean board.

Questions about the questions

Do I need an account?

No. QuizBlox has no accounts or servers. Everything runs in your browser.

Where are my question sets saved?

In your browser's local storage on this device. Clearing site data removes them, and they do not sync between devices.

Can students play against each other?

Not live. Every game is one player against simulated opponents, but Class mode lets a whole class play the same challenge and compare results through short codes.

Does it work on phones and tablets?

Yes. The layout adapts to small screens and all controls work by touch.

Can I pause a game?

Yes: press P or use the Pause button. The game also pauses by itself if you switch tabs. The question is hidden while paused, so no peeking.

Can it read questions aloud?

If your browser supports speech, switch on Read questions aloud in Settings, or press the 🔊 button on any question. Note that the question timer keeps running while it speaks.

What happens to questions I get wrong?

They go into a weak-spot bank in your browser. Use Practise weak spots on the Play page or after a game: no clocks, no bots, and each one leaves the bank when you get it right. You can also flip missed questions as Study cards from the end screen.

Can I revise a set without starting a game?

Yes. Open Study cards from the Play page or any set on the Question Sets page. Flip through the questions at your own pace, mark the ones you are still learning, and optionally practise just those.

Can I install it or use it offline?

Yes, when the site is served over http(s) (a school web server, GitHub Pages and so on). Your browser then offers to install QuizBlox as an app, and every page is saved for offline use, so games, sets, stats and Class mode keep working with no connection. Opening the files straight from a folder works too, just without installing. When the site is updated you get the new version the next time you open it.

Can I turn the sound off?

Yes. Use the Sound button on the Play page, or press M during a game. In Settings you can also set effects volume and music volume separately, or switch the background tune on.

How do I share my result?

After a game, a recap card shows your place, score, accuracy and best streak. Copy recap puts a short write-up on the clipboard, Share uses your device share sheet when it is available (and copies the text if not), and Save image downloads a picture you can post or send.

What is the question of the day?

A single question from a built-in set, picked for your local date and shown on the home page. There is no timer and no score. Your answer is remembered for the day, and a new question arrives tomorrow. Play this set opens a normal game with the same question set.

What can I see on the Stats page?

Besides your totals, play streak, personal bests and recent games, Stats now charts accuracy and place over your last 20 games, a form guide of recent places, a breakdown by game mode, and a this-week versus last-week snapshot. Everything stays in this browser.

Can two people play on one device?

Yes. On the Play page, tick Pass & Play (or open play.html?pass=1) and name both players. There are no bots: you take turns on the same screen. After each question a prompt tells you to pass the device, and the clock holds until the next player taps ready (or presses Space). Both scores stay on the HUD, and the end podium is just the two of you. Tick Team up against the bots (or open play.html?team=1) to share one score and take on the four rivals together, still passing the device between questions. Daily, weekly, class, demo, mix, tournament and challenge games stay one-player.

Is QuizBlox accessible?

Every page has a Skip to content link (visible when it is focused), labelled buttons, and dialogs that keep keyboard focus inside until you close them. The menu button announces Open or Close. High contrast, larger text, reduced motion, dyslexia-friendly spacing and optional read-aloud live in Settings.

What are the keyboard shortcuts?

Press ? on any page, or the keyboard button in the header, for a full list. In a game, 1–4 pick answers (and chests, moves or cards), P pauses, M mutes and Space is used in Vault Heist, Lucky Catch, and to ready the next player in Pass & Play. Opening the list mid-game pauses the clock so you can read it.

Can I try to beat my own score?

Yes. The Play summary shows your personal best for this set, mode, time and rules (hard mode and no-timers are kept separately). During a game a "Your best" pill tracks that score, and you get a toast if you pass it. Recently played sets also appear as chips above the set list so you can jump back in.

What is a photo finish?

If the clock runs out and you are tied for first with a bot, you get one extra sudden-death question. Get it right for +1 and first place; miss it and the tie stands (you finish second on the tie-break). There is no minigame on that question.

What do On Fire and Final Lap do?

Five correct answers in a row puts you On Fire (with bigger banners at 8 and 10). The last 15 seconds of a timed game are the Final Lap: a quicker beat, an urgent clock, and a heads-up if the race is close. Neither one changes how points are scored.

Is there a short demo for first-time players?

Yes. The intro tour, the Play page and the home page (when you have not played yet) offer a 30-second demo: a short Gold Quest with easy rivals and a couple of coach tips. Or open play.html?demo=1.

What is a classroom view?

On the Class mode page, Classroom view is a full-screen leaderboard for a projector: a podium, big names and scores, the class highlights, and a place to paste more result codes without leaving the board. Escape closes it. Reuse class starts a new challenge with the same set, mode, time and rules and an empty board; the previous class and its results stay put.

How do I paste a lot of questions at once?

In the editor, open Bulk add from text. A live preview appears as you paste. You can use pipe-separated lines (the first answer is correct, or mark one with *), CSV, A/B/C/D blocks, or GIFT (Question {=right ~wrong}, Statement {T}). The Import button on My sets also accepts a paste of questions, not just JSON, and saves them as a new set called “Pasted questions”.

What is the set of the week?

A rotating built-in set, given a weekly theme and a recommended mode. Play this week’s event from the home or Play page for a 3-minute game. Finishing it marks the card as done until the next week.

What is a lucky key?

In Gold Quest, every 5 answers in a row earns a lucky key (you can hold two). If you then open a dud chest, you can spend the key to reroll it into a good result, or keep the dud. The bots play by the same rule.

Is there a summer holidays game?

From November to January (summer in Australia), the home page offers a Summer holidays Quiz Race on the Summer Holidays set. Open play.html?season=1 any time in that window for a 3-minute race. It is a seasonal shortcut, not a fifth daily challenge.

Can I print a worksheet or an exit ticket?

Yes. On Question Sets, open a set and choose Print. You can print a student sheet, an answer key, both, or a five-question exit ticket (two copies on one page). Optional lines for class, teacher and period, difficulty marks, two-up sheets, and Version A/B (a shuffled second copy) are there if you want them, and Preview shows the page without printing. Class mode can still print a practise-missed worksheet as before.

What are altitude bands in Rocket Race?

As your rocket climbs, the track marks Launch pad, Upper air, Orbit and Space. Crossing a new band gets a short banner. It does not change how many kilometres a card is worth.

What is the all-modes tournament?

A playlist of all seven modes, 30 seconds each, against the same four rivals. Placement points from each leg (5 for 1st down to 1 for 5th) decide the winner, because gold, cash, kilograms and kilometres do not add up. Open play.html?tourney=1, or start it from the home or Play page. It counts as one game in your stats.

Can the rivals adapt to how I play?

Yes. On the Play page, set opponent difficulty to Auto. After you have a couple of recent results, rivals pick Easy, Normal or Hard to match your recent form. Each game still uses the usual bot formulas for that tier, so the published balance numbers do not change. You can switch back to Easy, Normal or Hard any time.
