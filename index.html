Why QuizBlox

Review time that doesn't feel like review time.

No setup, no logins, no downloads. Open it and play.

✏️

Your questions, your rules

Write multiple-choice, true/false and type-the-answer questions (with emoji pictures) in a clean editor with optional per-question timers. Duplicate, tweak, reuse. Nobody judges your typos.

🎮

Seven game modes

Gold Quest adds treasure chests and mild chaos, Quiz Race rewards speed, Vault Heist is a lock-picking caper, Lucky Catch is a fishing frenzy and Café Rush is a counter-service scramble, Monster Brawl is a team-up against a very large boss and Rocket Race is a card-powered climb to space. All come with opponents who never complain about homework.

📱

Runs on anything

Laptops, tablets, phones, that one Chromebook from 2016. Your sets stay saved in your browser between visits.

How it works

Three steps. Maybe four if you count snacks.

Game modes

Pick your flavour of competitive.

Same questions, different pace. Switch any time.

💰

Gold Quest

Chests, chaos, and a goblin or two. Every correct answer earns a chest pick.

  • Choose from three mystery chests
  • Win gold, double up, or steal from a bot
  • Beware: some chests are hungry
Play Gold Quest
🏁

Quiz Race

Fast fingers, big streaks. The more you nail, the more points you rack up.

  • Points for every correct answer
  • Streak and speed bonuses
  • First to the goal, or top score at the buzzer
Play Quiz Race
🔐

Vault Heist

A steady hand beats a lucky guess. Every right answer earns you a shot at the vault.

  • Stop the sliding marker in the green zone
  • Hit dead centre to swipe cash from a rival
  • Miss and the alarm costs you
Play Vault Heist
🎣

Lucky Catch

Hook it, reel it, brag about it. Bigger fish need faster fingers.

  • Mash the reel button before it escapes
  • Fish weigh from 1 kg to 25 kg (plus the odd boot)
  • Long streaks attract the big ones
Play Lucky Catch
☕

Café Rush

The queue is out the door and your memory is on trial. Right answers bring customers.

  • Tap items in the order the customer asks for
  • Patience bar ticking down, orders get longer
  • Tidy, speedy service earns big tips
Play Café Rush
👹

Monster Brawl

Everyone vs one very large boss. Right answers power your attacks, and the top damage dealer wins the glory.

  • Choose a safe jab, a risky smash or a power-up
  • Shared boss health bar, bots chip in too
  • Bosses get enraged and use telegraphed special attacks
  • Team brawl in Class mode: one boss for the whole class
Play Monster Brawl
🚀

Rocket Race

Fuel up with right answers, play your cards and race the bots’ rockets up to space. First to the top wins.

  • Boost for a jump, Shield to block, Sabotage the leader
  • Watch all five rockets climb a vertical track
  • Reach 100 km (space!) before the others
Play Rocket Race
🧑‍🏫

Class mode for teachers

Run one challenge for the whole class. No accounts, no server.

  • Share one link or a printed QR join card
  • Students send back a short result code
  • Leaderboard, most-missed questions, CSV and worksheets
